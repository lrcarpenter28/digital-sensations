1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by Digital Sensations through our mobile minidrama streaming application, our informational pages, customer support channels, and authorized social or promotional integrations (including TikTok Login, TikTok Minidrama promotional links, YouTube API Services channel publishing, and campaign attribution). Please also review our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Service, we may collect the following categories of information:

Category Examples of Data Collected Source
Account & Contact Data Optional email address, display name or username, user ID, language preference, and messages you send to customer support. Directly from you when registering or emailing support.
Third-Party / TikTok Login & API Data Only the basic profile attributes you explicitly authorize during OAuth sign-in (such as an open identifier / user ID, display name, or avatar URL). 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver & Personalize Streaming: Stream vertical video episodes reliably, remember your playback position, sync your watchlist, and recommend relevant minidrama series;
  • Manage Unlocks & Subscriptions: Maintain accurate Virtual Coin balances, verify VIP subscriptions, and restore unlocked chapters across devices;
  • Customer Support: Respond to billing inquiries, technical troubleshooting, copyright claims, and data privacy requests;
  • Analytics & App Stability: Measure episode engagement, monitor buffering or playback errors, and improve app performance;
  • Ad Measurement & Rewarded Ads: Attribute installs and promotional traffic from ad campaigns (including TikTok Ads and Apple SKAdNetwork) and credit Bonus Coins when you opt into rewarded video advertisements; and
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detect automated abuse, reward tampering, unauthorized screen-ripping, and payment fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Advertising, Analytics & Tracking Choices

You control how device identifiers and personalized advertising signals are used:

  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency): We request your explicit permission via the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before accessing your Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) for cross-app ad measurement. You can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization anytime in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • In-App & Website Controls: Where available in app settings, you can manage notification preferences and promotional tracking choices.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal contact information (such as your email address or name). We share information only in the limited circumstances below:

  • Service Providers & Hosting: Trusted infrastructure vendors who provide video content delivery networks (CDNs), cloud storage, authentication, crash reporting, and analytics strictly on our behalf under confidentiality obligations;
  • App Stores & Payment Processors: Apple, Google, or PCI-compliant billing partners to verify receipts, manage auto-renewals, and process refunds or fraud checks;
  • Advertising & Attribution Partners: Ad networks and measurement partners (such as TikTok Ads / SKAdNetwork or mobile measurement partners) to measure campaign performance and deliver optional rewarded ads, subject to your OS tracking permissions and applicable privacy law;
  • Legal & Safety Compliance: Governmental authorities or legal claimants when reasonably necessary to comply with valid legal process, protect our rights or users’ safety, or investigate fraud; and
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

6. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Account and Data

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Service, honor unlocked content and active subscriptions, prevent fraud, and satisfy legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

How to Delete Your Account & Data:
  1. In-App Deletion: Open the Digital Sensations app and go to Settings > Account > Delete Account (where available) to initiate immediate account deactivation and erasure.
  2. Email Request: Email eltacosbello@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request” and include the email address or account/open ID linked to your profile so we can verify ownership. We process verified deletion requests within 30 days.
  3. Revoking TikTok Authorization: If you signed in or connected via TikTok, you can revoke our access at any time inside TikTok via TikTok > Profile > Menu > Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions.
  4. Revoking Google / YouTube Authorization: If you authorized Digital Sensations via Google OAuth or YouTube API Services, you can revoke access at any time from your Google Security Settings page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
Note: Deleting your Digital Sensations account permanently removes your profile, Virtual Coin balance, and unlocked access, and does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription — remember to cancel auto-renewal in your device’s store settings.

Depending on where you reside (including under U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA, or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to:

To exercise any privacy right, email eltacosbello@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification before fulfilling requests.

8. Age Restrictions & Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent / 18 in your jurisdiction). If you believe a minor has provided personal information to Digital Sensations, please contact us immediately at eltacosbello@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the account and associated data.

9. Data Security & International Transfers

Digital Sensations operates from and primarily processes data in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate, subject to appropriate safeguards recognized by applicable law.
